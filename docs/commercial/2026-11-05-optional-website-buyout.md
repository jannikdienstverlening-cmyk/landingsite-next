# Optional website buyout

On 5 November 2026 the owner confirmed that the buyout is optional, that
EUR750 applies until 12 completed paid months (including months 6 to 12),
and that the following thresholds are 12, 18 and 24 months. The owner
approved publication for new customers with all amounts including VAT.

- The terms table reads from the buyout schedule in config/commercial.ts.
- The applicable tier is based on completed paid months of the subscription
  at termination, not on the date the customer submits the cancellation.
- Cancellation alone does not trigger a buyout, invoice or additional charge.
- Even a free transfer requires a separate written agreement. No third-party
  rights, new hosting commitment or migration work are implied.
- The new version is accepted at checkout. Existing agreements and their
  recorded terms versions are not retroactively changed.
- Stripe prices, VAT configuration, billing intervals and existing
  subscriptions are unchanged. There is no automated buyout payment.

The prior terms source is retained in Git at b1f46194. The existing written
transfer safeguards are retained; see the government guidance at
https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/wetten-en-regels/auteursrecht/.
